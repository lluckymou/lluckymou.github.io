CUSTOM BLOCKS

Wrap any sequence in [ ] to force it into a single block.
Use / inside to stack rows within the block.

[TH] T and H side by side in one block [TH/IS] TH on top, IS on bottom [W/OR/LD] three rows stacked in one block

APOSTROPHE

Apostrophes ( ' ) inside words act as syllable breaks - the apostrophe glyph leads the next syllable block.

don't → don · 't they're → they · 're it's → it · 's
